See it in action

Everything lives in one window, over the kaleidoscope it is playing.

Shape every pattern.

Eight shapes, from Classic to Fractal. Set the mirrors (or let them change on their own), scale and swirl, and turn on guide lines to see exactly where the mirrors fall. Or hit Randomize.

Pick what plays.

Your Photos albums and smart albums, folders on your Mac, and macOS's own wallpapers and aerials. Tick a whole album or single photos; nothing is copied or changed.

Keep the ones you love.

Bookmark a scene to save the photo with its pattern. Star your favorites, then play your scenes as a playlist.
