Your photos, as a slowly turning kaleidoscope.

Kaleido plays an animated background on your Mac's desktop, built from your Photos albums or macOS's slow-motion aerial films. Press Stop and your normal wallpaper is simply back.

Native Swift and Metal · macOS 26 or later · No accounts, no cloud

It never touches your wallpaper.

Playback happens in borderless windows just above the wallpaper and below your desktop icons, only while the app is open and you have pressed Play. Mouse clicks pass through.

✺

Photos albums & aerials

Pick albums, smart albums, folders or single photos. Or play macOS's own aerial films, with the 240 fps footage slowed about 8×. Your photos are never copied or modified.

❖

Scenes & Surprise me

A scene is a photo with a look: shape, mirrors, swirl, color. Save your favorites, or let Surprise me mix them with new ones Kaleido invents. Scenes last 90 seconds and cross-fade over 12.

⬡

Patterns & an editor

Built-in patterns such as Mandala, Whirlpool, Hex Garden, Prism and Spiral Tunnel across eight shapes, plus an editor to tune swirl, hue, saturation and dynamic mirrors.

◐

Hands on, slow by default

Drag the preview to move the photo inside the mirrors, flick it and it keeps gliding. Five speed stops from turtle to rabbit, and Push or Pull zoom.

❀

A good citizen

30 or 60 fps cap, pauses on battery if you like, pauses when the display sleeps or the screen locks, with a menu bar item for Play, Next and Surprise me. Works across Spaces and multiple displays.
